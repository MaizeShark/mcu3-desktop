## BRAKE / ESP telltales (done)
- `ESPStatusMessage::processMessage` (libQtCarVAPI, 0x145) only sets telltales with the drive rail
  on. ESP: On while `DI_tcTelltaleOn` (0x2B6 DI_chassisControlStatus) is on **or invalid**, or
  `ESP_espFaultLamp`; Flash with `ESP_espLampFlash`/`DI_*TelltaleFlash`. BRAKE: Red with
  `ESP_ebdFaultLamp`, brake fluid low/invalid or an EPB mismatch, otherwise Yellow unless
  `IBST_iBoosterStatus` (0x39D) is READY/ACTUATION (5/6). Enum values: Telltale Off/On/Flash,
  BrakesTelltale Off/Red/Yellow.
- Nobody sent 0x2B6 (the sim's drive inverter is patched out) or 0x39D, so both lit up. The
  `parked` preset of tesla-can.py now sends `DI_tcTelltaleOn=OFF`, `DI_ptcStateUI=ON`,
  `IBST_iBoosterStatus=READY`, `IBST_driverBrakeApply=BRAKES_NOT_APPLIED`. Verified with the
  rail on, parked and in D: `VAPI_telltaleESP=Off`, `VAPI_telltaleBrakes=Off`.

## More of the car (done)
- The sim covers doors, trunk/frunk, lock, charge port, charging, battery, lights, outside temp
  and tire pressures through `SIM_*` values (bools as true/false, enums by label). Table of what
  drives what: `vehicle/README.md`.
- Charge rate (mi/hr) = `GUI_chargeAveragePower` / Wh per mile, from `VAPI_batteryCurrent`
  (`BMS_packCurrent`, 0x132, **positive = charging**). The sim's dynamics model
  (`SimVehicle::updateDynamicsFromSim`) overwrites `SIM_batteryCurrent`/`SIM_powerLevel`, so it
  can't come from the sim.
- **Merge mode**: `QtCarSimService --udp :1235` (RuntimeEnv option, like `--udp :1234` of the
  other services; found at `RuntimeEnv::RuntimeEnv`, default port 0x4d2) sends to
  `tesla-can.py --sim-port 1235`, which forwards to :1234 with its own signals written into the
  sim's frames (checksum redone, mux branch checked). `start_all.sh` does this with `VEHICLE=1`.
- Presets (combinable, also at runtime with `--set --preset X`): drive, stop, rail-off, charging,
  charge-complete, unplugged, doors-open/-closed, locked/unlocked, lights-on/-off, tires-ok,
  tire-low, winter, summer.
- Web page: `tesla-can.py --http 8099` (start_all: http://localhost:8099/, `PANEL_PORT`).
  `vehicle/panel.html` + JSON API `/api/state`. Shows 22 live data values from QtCarVehicle.
- The sim persists its `SIM_*` values (`.Tesla/data/QtCarSimSettings.db`): doors left open stay
  open after a restart.
- Not done: TPMS warnings. The bits of `TPMS_StatusC` (0x36F) reach
  `TPMSStatusMessage::processContiMessage` -> `filterStatus(soft, ..., locations)`, but
  `TPMS_softWarnings` stays None even with learning/localisation PASSED. Filter has
  `TPMSDriveRailOnFilterTime` and `filterTpmsFaults()`; not followed further.
- "Touchscreen unresponsive / press and hold scroll wheels" alert in the sandbox without
  `--touch`: probably no touch device, not investigated.
