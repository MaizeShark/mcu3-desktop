# Session 7 (2026-09-25): CAN signals in the panel, the arcade

Wishes A and B of the session-6 prompt. Tested without sudo here and on the T480 laptop (sudo
there): `./tesla start --native --vehicle --audio`, touches injected into tesla-touch's device,
screenshots with `ffmpeg -f x11grab`.

## A. Every CAN signal on the panel
- `tesla-can.py` records the last frame of each CAN id it sees (`Sender.record`): its own frames
  (`set`), the simulator's forwarded ones (`sim`, merge mode), other senders on the port
  (`other`) and QtCar's requests on :4321 (`qtcar`; with `--http` the :4321 listener runs even
  without `--respond`). Per multiplexer branch: the branch is the value of the selector bits
  (`selector_bits`); zero-base signals that don't overlap any branch's bits are common and decode
  from the latest frame.
- `/api/db` (379 messages, 8759 signals with range/unit/enum/branch; 950 kB, 125 kB gzipped:
  `reply()` gzips when the client accepts it), `/api/can?ids=...` (all ids: age, rate, sender;
  for the given ids the frames and decoded signals).
- `panel.html`, section "CAN signals": search (all words must match: name, message, 0x id, unit,
  enum label), filters on the wire / set here / watched, lazy rendering (50 messages, "show
  more"), open messages poll `/api/can` every second (live values, frames), set/unset per signal
  (enum labels via datalist), ☆ pins signals (localStorage). `#TEXT` in the URL = a search.
- Checked with headless Chrome (screenshots desktop/phone width, CDP key events) and a fake
  sender; merge mode with the real simulator in the sandbox (podman: qtcar-sim + qtcar-vehicle).

## B. The arcade
What was wrong, one by one:
1. **No steering/scroll devices**: they aren't made by `input_to_virtual` (that one only makes
   `game-touch`) but by **QtCarVehicle**: `Model3VehicleLink::gameModeChanged` ->
   `SteeringInputForwarder::forwardInput(GUI_vehicleGameMode)` (libQtCarVAPI; the class is in
   libQtCarUIFramework) creates `game-scroll-left/right` and `game-steering` through /dev/uinput.
   It runs as tesla (uid 1111): "game-scroll-left Unable to open input device error: 13
   Permission denied" (laptop logs 20260925-115258). Fix: `./tesla start` gives uid 1111 an ACL
   on /dev/uinput (removed at the end).
2. **Their inputs** (found in the sandbox: qtcar-vehicle + tesla-can.py, reading 4030): see the
   table in `vehicle/README.md`. `VAPI_steeringAngle` <- `SCCM_steeringAngle`; `VAPI_brakePedal`
   is a DataValue with the source name `ETH_VCLEFT_brakePressed` -> `VCLEFT_brakePressed`;
   `STW_*` <- `VCLEFT_swc*`. `VirtualSteeringDevice::sendWheelAngle`: ABS_WHEEL = angle x 100
   (range +-82000). Sign: positive = right (checked twice: at +30 held for 2 s the car
   drove into the cliff on the right; QtCar shows "TOO MUCH RIGHT" at +40). The user's first
   "inverted" came from a panel tab with an older version; the panel now sends no-store. No accelerator: the game drives by itself.
3. **Device nodes**: the chroot's /dev/input is a tmpfs; the cobalt job now makes nodes for new
   `game-*` devices every 0.5 s while the game runs (before: once, 1 s after the start, and for
   every host device incl. the keyboard), and removes them and input_to_virtual when it ends.
4. **Software rendering**: "DRI2: could not open /dev/dri/card0 (Permission denied)", the
   GPU ACL was only for uid 1111. CobaltLinux ran at 640% CPU (llvmpipe), a few fps; taps of
   80 ms were lost (the game samples touches per frame), 0.8 s presses worked. Now cobalt (1226)
   and mame (1980) get the ACL too: 25% CPU.
5. **Touch offset**: `input_to_virtual` maps touches into the game window from the TIDK_WINDOW_*
   variables; the job started it with `env -i` without them. Now it gets them (x unchanged,
   y - 60 checked with strace).
6. **The window manager**: QtCar positions the game window (`ExternalAppView: repositioning
   window to 0 , 60 1920 x 1140`) and watches for it (`ExternalWindowConnection`, "BadAccess
   X_ChangeWindowAttributes": it wants the root's substructure redirect, the WM has it); xfwm4
   framed and placed it and the Xfce panel came up. `tools/game-windows.py` (ctypes Xlib, host,
   started by ./tesla start) takes such windows back: override-redirect, unmap, reparent to root,
   map; BBR2 at the TIDK geometry and kept on top; MAME below QtCar.
7. **MAME input**: QtCar (libTeslAtari: TeslAtariKeyboardDriver, MameWindow) sends keys with
   `XTestFakeKeyEvent`, i.e. to the focus window, which the WM gives to QtCar. The helper gives
   MAME's window the focus. COIN/START (QtCar's top bar) and the scroll wheels
   (`GUI_steeringWheelControlsMode` TeslAtari) then work. QtCar draws the MAME picture itself.
8. **Stopping**: the escalator runs `sv force-stop cobalt` and `sv DOWN mame` / `sv up mame`;
   the sv wrapper knew neither force-stop nor upper case, so games kept running after EXIT.
- Panel "Arcade controls": steering slider (springs back), hold-to-brake, both scroll wheels,
  keys (arrows/A/D/S/Space, after a click into the section), gamepad (Gamepad API). Quiet
  POSTs (`"quiet": true`: no log line, no state reply), one request at a time, latest wins.
- udev rule: `game-*` devices hidden from the host's libinput (else the desktop would take
  `game-touch` as a second touchscreen). `./tesla check` compares the installed rule.
- Injecting touches for tests: writing input_events to tesla-touch's event node works, but two
  taps on the same pixel lose their position (the kernel drops unchanged ABS values and the game
  starts a new touch at 0,0). tesla-touch itself already moves a new contact 1 px (differ_from).

Tested on the laptop: BBR2 menus by touch, a race: steering +-25/40 via the panel API, brake,
left wheel press, right tilt (power-ups); EXIT stops game + input_to_virtual, devices gone;
Asteroids: COIN, START, playing, high-score letters with the wheel. Not tested: sound (can't
listen; the game writes to tplug-media and QtCar selects source 0), VNC mode with the games,
MAME's other games, the gamepad (no gamepad here; the keyboard path was tested with CDP).

## Pump errors while BBR2 runs (solved: camera off during games)
The user's runs with the game (+ camera + Bluetooth): 21 and 79 audiod pump errors (earlier runs
0-3). Almost all `awe_in_a2b: Broken pipe` (capture overrun), each followed by audiod stopping
and restarting its streams (base amp too). audiod.log's `TXX`/`TIO` lines are slow pump cycles
(counter = cycle, 4 ms each): `aweio_pump_once` 6-9 ms, `snd_pcm_writei awe_out_base_amp`
blocking 4-8 ms; they rise from ~50 to 300-500 per 10 s while racing.
Tried on the laptop (live, 20-30 s windows, noisy because the game's load changes):
- camera ffmpeg paused (SIGSTOP): 0 errors vs 8-43 per 25 s, twice. But the input format (YUYV
  18 MB/s vs MJPEG), nice 19 or 1 thread made no clear difference.
- AudioWeaver's DSP thread already runs RR 35, audiod-pump RR 35: not starved by the game.
- the hrclock aplay at SCHED_FIFO 40, ksoftirqd at FIFO 50 (snd-dummy's hrtimer is a soft
  hrtimer), audiod+AWE pinned to the last core (3,7) with game/camera on the others (as the
  car's game-mode.sh isolates an audio core): no clear change.
Audible (user). Solved by turning the camera off during games: an A/B *during a race* (15 s
windows, camera SIGSTOP/SIGCONT) gave 0,4,3,3 errors with the camera vs 0,0,0,0 without, and a
run without camera had 0 while racing. Nice/threads/format didn't matter: probably memory
bandwidth (the iGPU renders in system RAM; ffmpeg converts and copies 49 MB/s of 1280x960 BGRX
frames into v4l2loopback), not CPU time. First version paused ffmpeg (SIGSTOP): after SIGCONT
it made up for the pause with 1000+ duplicated frames, and that burst gave 26 errors. Now the
camera loop kills ffmpeg while CobaltLinux/mametesla runs and starts a fresh one afterwards
(`camera.log`: "camera off while a game runs", `./tesla logs`: "off during N game(s)"). Result:
~5 errors while the game loads, 3 at the race start, then 0 per 30 s, 0 after EXIT.
Left after that (user's run 20260925-144456, camera off during the game, Bluetooth on): 6 errors,
1 at the game's start, then about one every 20-30 s of play (awe_in_a2b), "better, not perfect".
Ideas not tried: cap the game's frame rate (Mesa `vblank_mode`, or "ShowFPS" in the game's
DevConfig.json to see its rate first), a larger audiod buffer, Bluetooth off as a comparison.
Follow-up (Bluetooth off, camera configured): the game's "ShowFPS" in DevConfig.json does
nothing (release build). BBR2 doesn't render flat out: GPU at 500-900 of 1100 MHz, ~3 W, the
game ~50% CPU, so a frame cap has nothing to gain. After the laptop had cooled down (51-65 °C, no
thermal-throttle events): 0 pump errors in 130 s of racing, 20-80 slow cycles per 5 s. Earlier,
after hours of work: 140-260 slow cycles per 5 s, errors, once all cores at 800 MHz; the
throttle counters stood at 120k (core) / 172k (package) since boot. So what's left follows the
laptop's heat (T480s throttle), not something the setup does.

## Patch kit cleanup (2026-09-25 afternoon)
- 25 binary patches -> 5 (`utils-assert-nonfatal`, `cef-no-oscillator`, `sim-no-di-2hz/10hz`,
  `audiod-no-a2b`). New in the kit: `retired` per binary (firmware bytes, bytes an earlier kit
  wrote) and `RETIRED_FILES`; the kit restores/removes them where found, so an existing image
  ends up like a fresh one. `sha1_patched` is gone (per-patch checks only).
- Retired and why: `qtcar-cgroup-exit-*` (tested on the laptop with the firmware's bytes: the
  escalator puts QtCar into net_cls:/qtcar, no exit); `gui-escalator-*`, `gui-cgroup-exit-*`,
